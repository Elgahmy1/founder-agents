## 2. Arabic‑to‑English proposal translation assistant (score 3.4/5)

- Problem: Freelancers can outline their ideas in Arabic but lack a reliable way to convert them into professional English proposals without extensive manual editing.
- Customer: Egyptian freelancers who draft project outlines in Arabic and need a ready‑to‑submit English version for international platforms.
- Digital product: Web tool that accepts an Arabic draft and returns a polished English proposal, preserving intent and adding proposal‑specific structure.
- Alternatives today: Google Translate, ChatGPT (manual prompt for translation), Hiring human translators, Self‑editing after using generic translation tools
- Strongest alternative: Google Translate
- Why a customer would switch: Delivers proposal‑optimized English with contextual adjustments and Arabic explanations, reducing the need for time‑consuming manual edits.
- Scores: pain 2, founder fit 5, feasibility 4, reach 3, differentiation 3 (model judgement)
- Biggest risk: Translation quality may still fall short of professional standards, leading users to prefer human translators despite higher cost.

- Alternatives today: Upwork Help Center generic templates, Fiverr Learn course materials, Community‑shared templates in Facebook groups
