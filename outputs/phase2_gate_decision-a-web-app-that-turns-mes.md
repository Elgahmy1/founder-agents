# Phase 2 gate: demand_gate

**System recommendation: PIVOT**

Thresholds were fixed before the evidence was collected.

| Check | Target (set in advance) | Actual | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waitlist sign-ups (real, counted by the app) | >= 120 in 14 days | 0 | FAIL |
| Customer interviews completed | >= 15 | 2 | FAIL |
| Interviewees confirming the problem | >= 60% | 100% | PASS |

## Why
- People confirm the problem but did not sign up; pivot the offer, audience or channel.
- Failed: Waitlist sign-ups (real, counted by the app) (target >= 120 in 14 days, actual 0)
- Failed: Customer interviews completed (target >= 15, actual 2)

## Founder decision
- Decision: **GO**
