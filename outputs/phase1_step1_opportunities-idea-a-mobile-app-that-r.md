## 1. Hydration Quote Reminder App (score 2.4/5)

- Problem: Busy professionals forget to drink water during long work sessions, leading to mild dehydration and reduced focus.
- Customer: Desk‑bound professionals who spend many hours working on a computer.
- Digital product: A mobile app that sends push notifications with short motivational hydration quotes at customizable intervals.
- Alternatives today: Generic hydration reminder apps, Phone alarm clocks, Manual water‑tracking spreadsheets
- Scores: pain 2, founder fit 3, feasibility 3, reach 2, differentiation 2 (model judgement)
- Biggest risk: User fatigue from frequent push notifications and strong competition from free hydration apps.

Claims:
- If you forget to drink water while working for many hours, you may feel dehydrated and poorly; remembering to drink water makes you feel better. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40321368]
- **ASSUMPTION:** There are many existing hydration reminder apps and users often rely on phone alarms or manual tracking. _(no source)_

## 2. Workplace Hydration Dashboard (score 3.0/5)

- Problem: Office employees sit at desks for long periods and rarely receive water reminders, which can affect productivity and wellness.
- Customer: Office managers or HR teams seeking low‑cost wellness tools for their staff.
- Digital product: A web‑based widget that displays rotating motivational hydration quotes on office screens or intranet homepages, with optional email reminders.
- Alternatives today: Physical posters and flyers, Periodic email newsletters, General wellness programs
- Scores: pain 2, founder fit 3, feasibility 4, reach 3, differentiation 3 (model judgement)
- Biggest risk: Getting office decision‑makers to adopt a new digital widget when they already have low‑effort static solutions.

Claims:
- If you forget to drink water while working for many hours, you may feel dehydrated and poorly; remembering to drink water makes you feel better. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40321368]
- **ASSUMPTION:** Many offices rely on static posters or occasional emails to promote hydration, which are low‑engagement. _(no source)_

## 3. Team Hydration Bot (score 3.0/5)

- Problem: Remote team members often forget to drink water during virtual meetings, leading to fatigue and reduced concentration.
- Customer: Remote teams that use Slack or Discord for daily communication.
- Digital product: A bot that posts short motivational hydration quotes in a designated channel at configurable intervals.
- Alternatives today: Individual self‑reminders, Separate hydration apps, Manual check‑ins during meetings
- Scores: pain 2, founder fit 3, feasibility 4, reach 3, differentiation 3 (model judgement)
- Biggest risk: Bot messages may be ignored or muted if perceived as spam, limiting engagement.

Claims:
- If you forget to drink water while working for many hours, you may feel dehydrated and poorly; remembering to drink water makes you feel better. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40321368]
- **ASSUMPTION:** Remote teams typically rely on personal reminders or separate apps to stay hydrated. _(no source)_
