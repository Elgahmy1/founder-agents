# Phase 1, step 2: Define the market for 'Client‑Facing Weekly Dashboard'

- Ideal customer: Freelance professionals (e.g., developers, designers, consultants) who manage multiple client projects and need a quick, privacy‑preserving way to share weekly status updates.
- Segments: Solo software developers and engineers, Creative freelancers (design, writing, marketing), Consultants and agency‑affiliated freelancers, Gig‑economy remote workers who bill by the hour
- Where they gather: Reddit communities such as r/freelance and r/indiehackers, Hacker News and its comment threads, LinkedIn groups for freelancers and remote workers, Discord servers focused on freelancing and solo entrepreneurship
- Alternatives: Emailing PDF or Word reports, Shared Google Docs or Sheets, General project‑management tools with shared boards (e.g., Trello, Asana), Manual time‑tracking spreadsheets

## Claims
- Freelancers handle many communication channels (email, chat, voice notes) which adds workload beyond the core project tasks. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46076044]
- Freelancers often use time‑tracking tools to log work hours and analyze productivity. [source: https://workplace.stackexchange.com/q/10827]
- There are existing tools that automatically generate project reports from task and time data. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=39443060]
- Free project‑management tools are popular among remote workers and freelancers for organizing work. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45978877]
- Freelancers face challenges keeping up with client communications across multiple platforms. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46076044]

## Unknowns (to learn in interviews)
- How much are freelancers willing to pay for an automated weekly dashboard?
- Which time‑log formats (CSV, Toggl export, manual entry) are most commonly used?
- What level of client‑side access and privacy controls are required?
- Do freelancers prefer a standalone web app or integration with existing tools (e.g., Google Sheets, Notion)?
- How important is branding/customization of the client view to freelancers?
