# Phase 4, step 1: Pricing proposal (no payment is configured or taken)

Core features that must stay free (the primary user story, plus privacy and safety features): File Upload & Processing, Summary Generation, Result Display & Download, Automatic Data Deletion

| Tier | USD / month | Includes | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | File Upload & Processing, Summary Generation, Result Display & Download, Automatic Data Deletion, Practice Question Generation | Students who need occasional summaries and want full privacy protection |
| Pro | 9.99 | File Upload & Processing, Summary Generation, Result Display & Download, Automatic Data Deletion, Practice Question Generation | Students who want unlimited summaries and practice questions |

- InfoCaptor AI: Free Chrome extension with unlimited transcription but limited summarization [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45007478]
- **ASSUMPTION:** Whisperer AI: unknown _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Anki: unknown _(no source)_

Rationale: The free tier provides every CORE FEATURE so the product stays true to the founder’s values of accessibility and student privacy. It also lowers the barrier for adoption among students who may be skeptical of new tools. The Pro tier adds the convenience of unlimited usage and unlimited practice‑question generation, which are the primary value‑adds users are willing to pay for once they see the quality of the free version. Pricing at $9.99 / month aligns with existing study‑tool subscriptions (e.g., Quizlet Plus) and keeps the offering affordable for the target student market.

How to test willingness to pay without taking money: Build a simple landing page that lists the Free and Pro tiers, includes a brief description of the unlimited benefits of Pro, and adds a "Notify me when we launch" button for the Pro tier. Track how many email sign‑ups the button receives over a few weeks to gauge willingness to pay without collecting any money.
