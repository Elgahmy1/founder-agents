# Phase 1, step 2: Define the market for 'Workplace Hydration Dashboard'

- Ideal customer: Office managers or HR professionals responsible for employee wellness in small‑to‑medium sized companies
- Segments: Small and medium enterprises (10‑200 employees), Large corporations with dedicated wellness programs, Coworking space operators, Remote‑first companies that use shared digital dashboards
- Where they gather: LinkedIn groups for HR and employee wellness, Slack communities such as #HR‑Tech and #Wellness‑At‑Work, HR.com discussion forums, Industry newsletters like SHRM Connect, Twitter hashtags #HRTech and #Wellbeing
- Alternatives: Physical posters and flyers, Periodic email newsletters, General wellness programs, Standalone hydration reminder apps for individual employees

## Claims
- If you forget to drink water while working for many hours, you may feel dehydrated and poorly; remembering to drink water makes you feel better. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40321368]
- **ASSUMPTION:** Many offices rely on static posters or occasional emails to promote hydration, which are low‑engagement. _(no source)_

## Unknowns (to learn in interviews)
- What criteria do office decision‑makers use to approve a new digital wellness widget?
- How much budget are HR teams willing to allocate for a low‑cost hydration dashboard?
- What level of screen real estate or intranet integration is acceptable to IT departments?
- Do employees find rotating motivational quotes effective enough to change drinking behavior?
- Preferred frequency and channel (screen widget vs. email) for hydration reminders in the workplace?
