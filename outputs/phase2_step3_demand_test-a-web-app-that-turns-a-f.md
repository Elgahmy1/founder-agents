# Phase 2, step 3: Demand test plan (targets proposed BEFORE the test)

- Hypothesis: Freelancers experience significant pain creating weekly status reports manually and would adopt a tool that automates this process.
- Waitlist sign-ups target: 100 in 14 days
- Interviews target: 15
- Share confirming the problem: 50%
- Channels: r/freelance, r/indiefreelance, Freelance Discord servers (e.g., Freelance Hub, Indie Hackers), LinkedIn freelancer and remote‑work groups, Twitter/X (#freelance, #solopreneur), Hacker News
- Rationale: The founder has 10 h/week; allocating ~3 h/week to community outreach across the six listed channels is realistic. Assuming a 5% conversion from outreach impressions to waitlist sign‑ups (assumption), reaching ~2,000 freelancers yields the 100‑sign‑up target within a 14‑day window. From the waitlist we can recruit interviewees at an expected 15% conversion, giving 15 interviews. A pain‑confirm rate of 0.5 means at least half of interviewed freelancers must validate the problem and express willingness to pay, which is a meaningful threshold to decide whether to proceed.

Sign-ups are counted by the app from real form submissions. Interview results are entered by the founder.
The system never creates sign-ups.
