# Phase 6, step 3: Learning memo (the recommendation above is STOP and the memo cannot change it)

The Phase 6 learn_gate resulted in a STOP recommendation. All three pre‑set targets were missed: 0 store visits vs. >=200, 0 free sign‑ups vs. >=50, and only 1 user feedback vs. >=3. Paid orders were also 0 (target 5). The evidence shows insufficient demand, so stopping is the evidence‑based choice.

## What we learned
- No users visited the store during the test window, indicating no immediate interest.
- Zero free sign‑ups were captured, far below the target of 50, showing the value proposition did not attract sign‑ups.
- Only one user provided feedback, insufficient to validate the problem or solution.
- The prototype's mocked checkout did not generate any paid orders, reinforcing the lack of traction.

## Next steps
- Cease further investment in the current prototype and halt additional development.
- Conduct deeper qualitative customer discovery to understand the underlying reasons for lack of interest.
- Re‑evaluate the problem hypothesis and consider alternative solutions or market segments.
- Document the findings and share them with the founding team to inform future strategic decisions.
