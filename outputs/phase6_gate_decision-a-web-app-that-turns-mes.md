# Phase 6 gate: learn_gate

**System recommendation: STOP**

Thresholds were fixed before the evidence was collected.

| Check | Target (set in advance) | Actual | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store visits in the window | >= 200 | 0 | FAIL |
| Free sign-ups (counted by the app from real form posts) | >= 50 | 0 | FAIL |
| Users who wrote feedback | >= 3 | 1 | FAIL |

## Why
- Sign-ups reached 0% of target (under 25%) and feedback does not show the problem. Stopping now saves the founder's time and money.
- Paid orders: target 5, recorded 0. Checkout is MOCKED in this prototype, so paid orders are reported, not used for the recommendation.

## Founder decision
- Decision: **STOP**
