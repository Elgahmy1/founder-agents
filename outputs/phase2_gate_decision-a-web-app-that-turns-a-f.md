# Phase 2 gate: demand_gate

**System recommendation: PIVOT**

Thresholds were fixed before the evidence was collected.

| Check | Target (set in advance) | Actual | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waitlist sign-ups (real, counted by the app) | >= 100 in 14 days | 0 | FAIL |
| Customer interviews completed | >= 15 | 12 | FAIL |
| Interviewees confirming the problem | >= 50% | 50% | PASS |
