# Phase 1, step 2: Define the market for 'Arabic‑first AI proposal writer'

- Ideal customer: Arabic‑speaking Egyptian freelancer who seeks international projects on platforms like Upwork or Fiverr and feels insecure about writing English proposals.
- Segments: Web and mobile developers, Graphic designers and UI/UX designers, Content writers and translators, Digital marketers and SEO specialists
- Where they gather: Facebook groups for Egyptian freelancers (search for "Freelance Egypt" or "Egyptian freelancers" on Facebook), Discord servers dedicated to Egyptian freelancers (search Discord for "Egypt freelancers" or join the public server "MENA Freelance Community"), Reddit community r/Egypt (freelance‑related threads) and r/freelance (filter for Egypt‑related posts), Local meet‑up events listed on Meetup.com under "Freelance" in Cairo or Alexandria
- Alternatives: ChatGPT (free general‑purpose AI writer), Upwork Help Center proposal guidelines (English only), Ad‑hoc templates shared in Facebook freelancer groups, Generic English‑only proposal templates found via Google

## Claims
- **ASSUMPTION:** Egypt has a growing community of freelancers on international platforms such as Upwork and Fiverr. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Many Egyptian freelancers report difficulty crafting persuasive English proposals. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Existing AI writing tools (e.g., ChatGPT) are used for proposal drafting but provide no Arabic explanations. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Freelancers often rely on informal Facebook groups to exchange proposal templates and tips. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Arabic‑first guidance could reduce the time spent translating ideas into English. _(no source)_

## Strongest counter-evidence (searched for on purpose)
- **ASSUMPTION:** Free general‑purpose AI tools like ChatGPT already generate English proposals, reducing the need for a paid specialized product. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Freelancers may be reluctant to pay for a niche tool when they can obtain templates and advice for free in Facebook groups. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Low average disposable income among Egyptian freelancers could limit willingness to spend on SaaS subscriptions. _(no source)_

## Unknowns (to learn in interviews)
- Would Egyptian freelancers be willing to pay for an Arabic‑first proposal writer, and at what price point?
- How much value do freelancers place on Arabic explanations of phrasing versus using free English‑only tools?
- What specific features (e.g., tone selection, cultural nuance, template library) are most important to them?
- How do freelancers currently translate their ideas into English proposals (manual translation, copy‑pasting, using ChatGPT, etc.)?
