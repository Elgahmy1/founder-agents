# Phase 2, step 3: Demand test plan (targets proposed BEFORE the test)

- Hypothesis: STEM undergraduate students will sign up for a waitlist and confirm that they struggle with turning lecture PDFs/slides into exam‑ready summaries and practice questions, indicating demand for an AI‑powered web app.
- Waitlist sign-ups target: 120 in 14 days
- Interviews target: 15
- Share confirming the problem: 60%
- Channels: Reddit (r/college, r/EngineeringStudents, etc.), Discord servers for university courses and study groups, University online forums/LMS (Canvas, Blackboard), Twitter/X posts with student hashtags, Word‑of‑mouth referrals from tutoring peers
- Rationale: With 10 hours/week the founder can allocate ~5 hours to outreach (posting, engaging) and ~5 hours to building the waitlist landing page. Free channels (Reddit, Discord, university forums, Twitter) can collectively reach an estimated 500‑800 students in the 2‑week window (assumption). Assuming a modest 20 % conversion from exposure to waitlist sign‑up (assumption) yields ~100‑160 signups; setting the target at 120 ensures the test is meaningful and would change the decision if missed. From the waitlist, interviewing 15 users (≈10 % of sign‑ups) is feasible within the time budget and provides enough qualitative insight. A pain‑confirm rate of 60 % (assumption based on typical early‑stage interest in study‑aid tools) is chosen; falling below this would indicate the core problem is not strong enough to pursue. All targets are set before any data collection and are high enough that missing them would prompt a pivot or stop decision.

Sign-ups are counted by the app from real form submissions. Interview results are entered by the founder.
The system never creates sign-ups.
