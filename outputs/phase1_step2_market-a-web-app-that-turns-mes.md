# Phase 1, step 2: Define the market for 'Exam‑Ready Summaries & Practice Questions'

- Ideal customer: Undergraduate STEM student at a university who takes lecture notes or receives slide decks and wants concise, exam‑focused summaries and practice questions.
- Segments: First‑year STEM undergraduates who are still developing effective note‑taking habits, Upper‑classmen preparing for midterms or final exams in large lecture courses, Students in majors with heavy quantitative content (e.g., engineering, physics, computer science), Students who rely on digital PDFs or slide decks rather than handwritten notes
- Where they gather: Reddit communities such as r/college, r/learnprogramming, r/EngineeringStudents, Discord servers for university courses or study groups, Facebook groups for specific university classes or majors, University‑run online forums and learning management systems (e.g., Canvas, Blackboard) where students discuss study resources
- Alternatives: Manually rewriting and reorganizing lecture notes, General video/lecture summarizers (e.g., InfoCaptor AI), Automatic transcription apps (e.g., Whisperer AI), Flashcard apps like Anki, Quizlet or other crowd‑sourced study‑set platforms

## Claims
- Students spend a lot of time watching lectures and extracting key insights, which they find tedious. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45007478]
- There is demand for tools that automatically transcribe and summarize lecture content. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46025891]
- Existing tools like InfoCaptor AI focus on video summarization and do not produce exam‑ready summaries or practice questions. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45007478]
- Automatic meeting/lecture transcription apps aim to make capture effortless but are not tailored to exam preparation. [source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46025891]
- **ASSUMPTION:** Undergraduate STEM students constitute a major portion of university enrollment in the United States. _(no source)_
- **ASSUMPTION:** Students frequently use Reddit and Discord to share study resources and discuss exam preparation. _(no source)_
