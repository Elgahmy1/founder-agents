# Phase 1, step 4: Co-founder verdict on 'Arabic‑first AI proposal writer'

**Verdict: DO NOT PURSUE.** This is weak: we have no sourced proof that Egyptian freelancers need or will pay for an Arabic‑first proposal writer.

_Capped in code from WEAK to DO_NOT_PURSUE: the evidence gate said STOP and the best opportunity scored 3.6/5._

## Why it could work
- Founder is fluent in English and Arabic and can build the LLM‑based web app himself (founder_fit=5).
- The target market (Egyptian freelancers) is sizable and active online, giving decent reach potential (reach=4).
- Existing tools lack Arabic explanations, creating a clear differentiation angle (differentiation=3).

## Why it could fail
- No independent sources confirm the pain or willingness to pay (evidence_gate failures).
- Free alternatives like ChatGPT already generate English proposals, reducing perceived value of a paid tool.
- Egyptian freelancers have low disposable income, making subscription pricing risky.

**Most likely to kill it:** Unvalidated demand – we don’t know if freelancers will actually pay for Arabic‑first guidance over free English‑only tools.
