# Phase 1, step 4: Co-founder verdict on 'Git‑Commit Weekly Summary Generator'

**Verdict: DO NOT PURSUE.** The idea is weak as stated: freelancers can already use free AI or manual templates, and we have no evidence of real pain or willingness to pay in Egypt.

_Capped in code from WEAK to DO_NOT_PURSUE: the evidence gate said STOP and the best opportunity scored 3.4/5._

## Why it could work
- Founder has strong Python and LLM development skills (fit = 5).
- Technical feasibility is high; building a Git‑OAuth + LLM pipeline is doable within $150 and 12 h/week.

## Why it could fail
- No sourced evidence that Egyptian freelancers struggle with weekly reporting or would pay for an English‑only tool.
- Free alternatives (ChatGPT, manual templates) already meet the need at zero cost.
- Potential language barrier: Arabic is the primary business language in Egypt, increasing localisation cost beyond budget.

**Most likely to kill it:** Lack of willingness to pay because free AI assistants already solve the summarisation problem.

## How to make it stronger

| Change | Why | Cheapest test this week |
|---|---|---|
| Add Arabic language support for the generated report and UI. | Mitigates the language barrier risk and expands the addressable market in Egypt. | Create a simple bilingual template and post it in the "Egyptian Software Developers" LinkedIn group asking for feedback (2 h, $0). |
| Target a narrower niche: developers who already use a specific project‑management tool (e.g., ClickUp) and need automated weekly status sync. | A focused wedge reduces competition from generic AI and creates a clear integration advantage. | Reach out to 5 ClickUp power‑users in Egypt via Telegram, offer a free prototype and ask if they'd use it (2 h, $0). |
| Offer a free tier that generates a report but requires manual export of commit logs, and a paid tier that handles OAuth integration. | Allows validation of demand without upfront cost, and the paid tier can be justified if users see time savings. | Build a minimal web page with a textarea for pasting commit logs and a button that calls the LLM (3 h, $20 for OpenAI API credits). Share the link in r/freelance and measure sign‑ups (5 h, $20). |
| Validate willingness to pay with a price‑point poll instead of a sales pitch. | Directly addresses the biggest unknown – how much Egyptian freelancers would pay. | Create a Google Form with three price options (e.g., $5, $10, $15/month) and share it in the Upwork Egypt freelancer search results (2 h, $0). |

## When to launch

- **Best window:** 5‑19 December 2026
- **Why:** Freelancers are active before the year‑end holiday slowdown, and the founder has 2 weeks (24 h) to prepare the minimal prototype and run the tests. This window avoids the major Egyptian holidays (Eid al‑Fitr in early June and Eid al‑Adha in late July).
- **Avoid:** none

**What would change my mind:** If we obtain at least two independent, credible sources showing Egyptian freelancers regularly spend >2 h/week on manual progress reports and express willingness to pay $5+ for automation, the verdict would move to "promising".
