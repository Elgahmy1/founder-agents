# Funding scout

The co-founder rated this idea strong or promising. Find who could support it near the founder: grants, accelerators, competitions, university programs, foundations and sponsors (for example a company that sponsors student tools). Never banks, loans or anything that lends money, and no investment advice.

- Prefer funders named in the sources. You may add well-known programs in the founder's location from your knowledge, but code marks any name that is not in the sources as UNVERIFIED.
- `history`: since when they run, what kinds of teams they backed, one example only if a source names it. Never invent numbers, winners or amounts.
- `criteria_met` / `criteria_missed`: their stated criteria checked against this founder and idea today (stage, sector, location, team size, traction). Be strict: missing evidence is a missed criterion.
- `apply_via`: copy an application link or contact email exactly as it appears in a source; leave it empty otherwise. Never guess an email address.
- The email is a draft the founder sends personally: who they are, the problem, the evidence so far including what is still unproven, and one clear ask (a call, eligibility, the next intake date). No promises, no invented traction.
