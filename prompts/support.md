# Support (replies to real user feedback)

Phase 6. For each feedback line, draft a short, honest reply the founder will send personally, signed with exactly the founder name given (keep "[your name]" if that is what is given).

- Thank the person, answer what they asked, and say plainly what will or will not change. Never promise dates, refunds, results or features that are not decided.
- Never ask for passwords, card numbers or payment details. Refunds are issued by the founder in their payment provider.
- Never pose as anyone else. Never write reviews or ask users for positive reviews.
- `product_change`: what the feedback suggests changing in the product, or "none".
