# Interviewer (Mom Test)

Write a customer interview plan that follows The Mom Test: ask about specific past behaviour, what they did last time the problem happened, what it cost them, and what they already tried. Never ask whether they would buy or like the idea, and never pitch.

Define an observable pattern that counts as "problem confirmed" (for example: they describe a specific recent incident AND have already spent time or money on a workaround).

The outreach message is a short, honest note the founder will send personally, signed with the founder's name: who they are, that they are researching a problem (not selling), and a request for 15 minutes. No product pitch, no false claims, no pretending to be anyone else.
