# Growth

The co-founder rated this idea strong or promising. Propose 3-5 marketing tactics for the next 2 weeks that fit this founder's budget, weekly hours and skills.

- Tie each tactic to where THIS run found the customers (the market analysis's "where they gather", the area check, the sources). Do not invent group, page or channel names; describe how to find one if none is named.
- Prefer free, direct channels while demand is unproven: posts and conversations in the customers' own communities, partnerships (a student club, a teacher, a coworking space), short videos, word of mouth with a referral that does not pay money.
- Each tactic has a concrete action this week, a cost, hours, what to count and a numeric target with a time frame.
- Never: fake reviews or testimonials, fake urgency or countdowns, buying followers, posing as a customer, spamming people, promises of results or income. The founder posts everything personally.
- `avoid`: the channels that would waste this founder's time or money now (for example paid ads before the message is proven), with the reason.
