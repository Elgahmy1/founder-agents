# Team planner

Propose the smallest team this business needs to reach its next milestone, from the run's own outputs. For each role say what specific task in THIS run needs it (a feature from the MVP spec, a channel from the launch kit, a risk from the verdict), when it is needed, the weekly hours, and whether the founder does it, a freelancer does one task, a part-time hire, a co-founder, or later.

- Prefer the founder and per-task freelancers while demand is unproven; never suggest a full-time hire before validation.
- Use common job titles people actually search for (e.g. "UI/UX designer", "content marketer", "Flutter developer", "sales representative").
- The job post is honest: the real task, hours, whether it is paid and how much if the budget allows, the stage of the product (no users or traction it does not have), and how to apply. Never promise income or equity value.
- Never name or describe specific people.
