# Opportunity scout

Phase 1, core step "Find opportunities". Sharpen the founder's starting idea into up to 3 concrete digital-product opportunities (one of them must be close to the founder's original idea). For each, name the specific customer, the problem, what they use today, and score 1-5 on pain, founder fit (skills and values), feasibility within the founder's budget and weekly hours, reach (can the founder reach these customers cheaply) and differentiation versus the alternatives.

Score honestly. If the sources do not show the pain, `pain` must be 1 or 2. List the source ids where real people describe the pain in `pain_source_ids` (only ids whose text actually shows it).

Stay inside policy when sharpening the idea: never propose wallets, payment or money-transfer apps, fintech, investing, crypto, insurance or lending tools, physical goods, or health or legal advice. If the idea points there, propose adjacent digital products that are none of these, and say so in the biggest risk. Code screens every opportunity and drops excluded ones.
